THE BOTANICAL LINEAGE

Honouring the Fragile Petal that Defined West Javanese Royalty

Unlike commercial relishes blended with high-speed blades and artificial preservatives, PRIMA QOE preserves the historic culinary sanctity of Bunga Kecombrang. The botanical bud is sliced sheerly by hand, preserving its natural floral astringency, peppery notes, and delicate essential oils.

Infused in cold-pressed virgin coconut oil, braised with Brebes heirloom shallots, garlic, and highland chilies, our recipe pays solemn homage to our family's heritage recipes dating back three generations in Priangan highlands.
